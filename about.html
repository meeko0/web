About Me

Who I Am

I'm Michael, a Computer Science student at Georgia State University with a focus on frontend development, user experience design, and AI/HCI research. Graduating December 2026, I'm heading to Georgia Tech's Master's program in August 2027. I'm driven by clean code, intentional design, and building products that people actually want to use.

What I Do

I lead frontend development on projects ranging from student housing platforms to design systems. I've worked as a frontend engineer at Georgia Hybrid Batteries and in full-stack roles at Dynamic Active. Currently, I'm a UX Design intern at Roadie (a UPS company) and serve as VP of the Google Developer Group at GSU. I'm also an undergraduate researcher in HCI and AI at SCAD's research lab, exploring how AI and human-centered design intersect.
